Endometriosis, characterized as an enigmatic disease\footnote{No known biomarkers, no cure, no standards for treatment: "Benign, common, but controversial disease due to its enigmatic etiopathogenesis and biological behavior\cite{Takeuchi2024Endometriosis}"}, is a chronic, inflammatory condition with a heterogenous set of symptoms that affects $\sim$6-10\% of women of reproductive age\cite{Zondervan2018Endometriosis}. Qualitative research that engaged both patients and providers has described several challenges with addressing this condition\cite{Pichon2021Divided,Armour2019Endometriosis,Mardon2023EndometriosisReview,OHara2019EndometriosisReview}. There are two allied challenges germane to this project:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Patients experience day-to-day uncertainty with managing pain, flare-ups, emotional and physical discomfiture}, have complex \textit{inexpressed} or \textit{underexpressed} experience, and no condition-specific tools to make sense of their lived embodied experience with the condition\footnote{``\textit{It becomes its own full-time job.}''\cite{Pichon2021Divided}}.
  \item \textbf{Patients and their care providers employ different temporal resolutions}: patients live day-to-day (or moment-to-moment) whereas providers think visit-to-visit. Further, patients also described intense emotional and cognitive preparation prior to clinical encounters, worrying about both \circled{i} dismissal and \circled{ii} how to present evidence of their suffering within a short, circumscribed amount of time\footnote{``\textit{It feels like a trial, I have seven minutes as a lawyer to prove my case, to present enough evidence, to hit the particular buzzwords.}''\cite{Pichon2021Divided}}
\end{enumerate}

% \subsection{Current State of Informatics Interventions}

Current tools address some aspects of these problems but offer critical interventional opportunities.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Self-tracking apps enable data collection but provide limited support for sensemaking or insight generation: there is a mistranslation between a patient's embodied, `messy', emotional experience and structured fields that offer very few avenues for reflection, sensemaking, and insight generation.
  \item Timeline visualizations support analyst and provider decision-making processes but do not incorporate the lived experience of the patient and/or their narrative\cite{Ledesma2019HealthTimeline,Rind2013InteractiveEHR}.
\end{enumerate}

Conversational AI for personal health management is emergent\cite{Rind2013InteractiveEHR} but has not yet been applied to, nor studied in, enigmatic conditions, or integrated with temporal visualization.

In short, \textit{there is no existing tool that bridges health expression between the conversational and the computational that emerges patient authored, clinically useful artifacts.}
